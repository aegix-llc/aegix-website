Our Team

Dr. Ying Wang

Co-Founder

Dr. Wang is an Associate Professor in the Department of Systems Engineering at Stevens Institute of Technology, where she directs the Mobile AI Cybersecurity Computing (MACC) Lab. Her research covers AI-driven 5G/NextG security, resilient wireless communications, and dynamic assurance for cyber-physical and autonomous systems. Her work is supported by the National Science Foundation, DARPA, the U.S. Army Research Office, the Department of Defense and the Defense Logistics Agency.

Dr. Juntao Chen

Co-Founder

Dr. Chen is an Associate Professor of Computer and Information Sciences at Fordham University, where he directs the Security and Intelligence (SAIL) Lab. His research covers the security and resilience of autonomous and cyber-physical systems, reinforcement learning, trustworthy AI, and quantum computing security. His work is supported by DARPA and the National Science Foundation.
